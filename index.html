01 — Operating principles

Discipline is structural, not emotional.

AXIOM 1

Truth enables correction

If it cannot be measured, stress-tested, or logically examined, it does not dictate direction. Truth → correction → adaptation → performance.

AXIOM 2

Actions before feelings

I do not wait to feel ready. Execute the plan, then evaluate the output. Feelings are signals — not commanders.

AXIOM 3

Trends over impulses

Longitudinal data over single bad days. Metrics over mood. If something fails, I don't collapse — I debug.

AXIOM 4

Consistency compounds

Wake up. Train. Log. Eat. Recover. Study. Repeat. No drama, no mythology. Compounding is law.

AXIOM 5

The body is a system

HRV is feedback. Lactate threshold is a variable. Sleep is the repair protocol. Undereating is self-sabotage.

AXIOM 6

Precision over impression

I am not here to be impressive. I am here to be precise — and, through precision, formidable.

∎  Discipline and truth is the way. I walk it daily.
