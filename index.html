01 — Principle

Leave room to be wrong.

A proof needs to hold up line by line. Code needs to do what I say it does. A training log needs to leave the missed weeks in. I try to bring the same care to all three.

When something doesn’t work, I want to understand why before I try again.

That is why I keep the source and the training record close to the story. They give me a way to notice when the story needs changing.

truth → correction → adaptation

02 — Academic

Math is where I learn how not to hand-wave.

B.Sc. Mathematics at İYTE. The attraction is not abstraction for its own sake; it is the discipline of statements that either hold or do not.

The recurring question: what stays invariant when the surface changes? That points me toward topology and knot theory.

The applied side is machine learning and large language models: systems that look like language, break under pressure, and need careful evaluation before anyone should trust them.

topology · knot theory · ML · LLM evaluation
